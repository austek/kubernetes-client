### Plain-language verdict

Of the 74 published modules, the Task 2 root-pom bnd config reaches 59 with no further
change: every module that already declares packaging=bundle gets its main jar's
module-info injected for free, because bnd's Jar object for that execution IS the main
artifact.

It buys nothing, unmodified, for the other 15 packaging=jar modules. Of those 15,
9 are core runtime artifacts that DO run bnd -- all five httpclient implementations
(httpclient-jdk, httpclient-jetty, httpclient-okhttp, httpclient-vertx, httpclient-vertx-5)
plus kubernetes-client-api, kubernetes-client, openshift-client-api and openshift-client --
but bnd's Jar object for them is the classified -bundle execution, not the main jar
consumers actually depend on. Converting packaging=jar to packaging=bundle fixes this:
Step 6's prototype on kubernetes-client-api shows the main jar gaining a real
"open module io.fabric8.kubernetes.client.api" descriptor with its exports and transitive
requires graph intact, and the -bundle classified jar disappearing from target/ entirely --
one artifact does the job that took two.

The remaining 6 of the 15 (junit/kube-api-test/client-inject, junit/kube-api-test/core,
junit/kubernetes-junit-jupiter, junit/kubernetes-server-mock, junit/mockwebserver, and
extensions/open-virtual-network/client) run no maven-bundle-plugin execution at all, in
any form. The packaging=bundle remedy is irrelevant to them; they are simply outside bnd's
reach regardless of packaging and would need a bnd execution added from scratch, or a
different mechanism (moditect), to get a descriptor.

The cost of applying the remedy to the 9 core artifacts: packaging=bundle is a
consumer-visible change to the published artifact's Maven packaging, and it removes a
currently-published classified artifact. Concretely, platforms/karaf/features/src/main/
resources/feature.xml resolves three of the nine (kubernetes-httpclient-jdk,
kubernetes-client, openshift-client) via mvn:.../${project.version}/jar/bundle coordinates
that assume the classified jar exists, and platforms/karaf/itests/pom.xml declares two
<classifier>bundle</classifier> dependencies on the same assumption. Both would need
updating (dropping the classifier / the /bundle suffix) before any of the 9 could convert.
10 poms in the whole repo declare <classifier>bundle</classifier> today: the 9 producers
plus this one consumer -- not the 11 the brief assumed; recount before relying on that
figure elsewhere in the spike.

Net: bnd-with-central-config, unmodified, silently does nothing for the main jar of the
three packaging=jar modules in this task's slice (and six of the other packaging=jar
modules get nothing under any packaging). Fixing the nine core artifacts is a real,
demonstrated fix, but it is a packaging change with two concrete downstream call sites to
update, not a config-only tweak -- it cannot ride inside a mechanism spike and would need
its own review.
